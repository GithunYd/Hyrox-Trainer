01FoundationFind your rhythmOct 5 – Oct 110/7+

Monday · Oct 5

Strength foundations

Strength40–50 min
  1. Warm up: 5 min brisk walk or easy bike, then 10 bodyweight squats, 10 alternating reverse lunges and 10 arm circles each way.
  2. Goblet squat 2 × 8; dumbbell Romanian deadlift 2 × 8; incline push-up 2 × 8; one-arm dumbbell row 2 × 10 per side; standing calf raise 2 × 12; plank 2 × 25 sec (rest 45 sec).
  3. Choose a load that leaves 2–3 good reps in reserve (RPE 6–7). Rest 90 sec between sets. Increase by the smallest available increment only when all reps are controlled; keep loads lighter during recovery and taper weeks.
  4. Cool down: 5 min easy walk, then 30 sec each calf, hip-flexor and chest stretch per side.

  3. Cool down: 5 min easy walk, then 30 sec each calf, hip-flexor and chest stretch per side.

Wednesday · Oct 7

Station skills

Stations36–40 min
  1. Warm up: 5 min brisk walk or easy bike, then 10 bodyweight squats, 10 alternating reverse lunges and 10 arm circles each way.
  2. 2 rounds, in this order: SkiErg 200 m; sled push 12.5 m; sled pull 12.5 m; burpee broad jumps 10 m; row 200 m; farmers carry 40 m; sandbag lunges 10 m; 8 wall balls. Rest 60 sec between stations and 2 min between rounds.
  3. Use light technique loads at RPE 5–6. Start lunges with bodyweight and wall balls with a light ball. Use a measured lane. For sleds, choose a load you can move smoothly; surface friction matters more than a fixed gym weight.
  4. No equipment? Replace each SkiErg/row with 2 min brisk incline walk; each sled push with 45 sec incline walk; each sled pull with 12 cable or resistance-band rows; carry with two dumbbells; sandbag lunges with bodyweight; wall balls with squat-to-press. These build fitness, but practice actual equipment before racing.
  5. Cool down: 5 min easy walk, then 30 sec each calf, hip-flexor and chest stretch per side.

Consistency beats intensity.

Make it your level

Designed as a starting plan for a generally healthy beginner who can brisk-walk 30 minutes. Run/walk is welcome. Repeat a week or reduce volume if you are not recovering. Don’t cram missed sessions into the next day.

Find the right effort

RPE is effort out of 10. Easy: 3–4, full sentences. Steady: 5–6, short sentences. Strength: leave 2–3 good reps in reserve. Stop if pain changes your movement; chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness need prompt medical attention.

Recover to progress

Thursday is full rest. Sunday is optional recovery. Weeks 4, 8, 12 and 16 reduce work; weeks 20–21 taper. Sleep, familiar meals and normal hydration support the training. Get individual guidance if you have an injury or health condition.

This is an original general beginner plan created for this app from the requested schedule, not a recovered coach-authored plan or an official HYROX program. Five training days per week; scale them to your starting fitness.

4 × 12.5 m. Stay inside your assigned box. Keep rope clear; move the entire sled past the line.

0480 m

Burpee broad jumps

Chest touches the floor. Jump forward with both feet taking off and landing together; follow the marked lane.

051,000 m

Row

Legs, body, arms; reverse on recovery. Use a sustainable stroke rate and confirm the full distance.

06200 m

Farmers carry

Carry two kettlebells by your sides. You may put them down to rest; don’t gain distance without them.

07100 m

Sandbag lunges

Bag across both shoulders. Rear knee touches the floor; extend hips and knees fully at the top.

08100 reps

Wall balls

Squat below parallel, then hit the correct target. Break into manageable sets and listen for no-reps.
